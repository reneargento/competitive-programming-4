6.5.5.4

To find the repeated substring that occurs the most (and between possible candidates, the longest one), the following strategy can be used:
Find the largest consecutive range of k suffixes whose minimum LCP value is as large as possible.

For LCP[i..j], the common prefix of the suffixes SA[i..j + 1] has length:
min(LCP[i..j])
The longest repeated substring that occurs the most will be in T[SA[i] .. SA[i] + minLCP]
